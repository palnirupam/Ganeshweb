01/STILLNESS

May every beginning be blessed.

A LITTLE STILLNESS. A LITTLE MAGIC. An ode to the remover of obstacles Made of light & love ✧
THE INTENTION ✧

Every beginning
deserves a little light.

Aarambh means beginning. This is a small tribute to Ganesh, the remover of obstacles, and to the quiet courage it takes to begin again.

One touch sets the light in motion. Stay as it finds its form. Let this moment be yours.

Shubho hok protita notun shuru.
